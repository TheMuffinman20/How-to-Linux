# Introduction

In this workshop, you follow a fixed step-by-step plan of instructions, supplemented with explanations and background information so that you understand exactly what we are doing and why some choices are important. The goal is to install Linux on your computer, either alone or alongside Windows, and also to show why Linux is a powerful and high-performance operating system.

**Estimated duration:** 90 to 120 minutes

**Requirements**
- A USB stick of at least 8 GB
- A stable internet connection (WiFi or Ethernet)

> [!WARNING]
> Before you begin, make sure to back up Windows (or your current operating system) and all important files.

> [!CAUTION]
> All data currently on the USB stick will be completely erased and cannot be recovered.
